About Solaris Lanka

We're on a mission to make clean, affordable solar energy accessible to every Sri Lankan home and business.

Our Story

A Decade of Powering Progress

Since 2014, Solaris Lanka has installed over 500 solar systems across the island — from small family homes to industrial-scale commercial arrays. What began as a small team of engineers in Colombo has grown into one of Sri Lanka's most trusted renewable energy partners.

We believe energy independence shouldn't be a luxury. That's why we combine global-quality products with local expertise to deliver systems that pay for themselves — and then keep paying you back.

Our Mission

To accelerate Sri Lanka's transition to renewable energy by delivering world-class solar systems at fair prices.

Our Vision

A future where every rooftop in Sri Lanka is a power plant — clean, independent, and profitable.

Our Values

Integrity, craftsmanship, transparency, and unwavering commitment to every customer we serve.
